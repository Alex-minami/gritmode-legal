3.6. Analytics

GritMode 1.1.0 and later use Amplitude (operated by Amplitude, Inc., United States — https://amplitude.com/privacy) for product analytics. If analytics is configured in a build, the SDK may send a pseudonymous Amplitude device identifier, app and device details (including app/build version, device model, operating system, carrier, session events, and approximate country derived from network information such as IP address), and product events.

Current product events include app opens; onboarding progress and completion; selected-app counts (not app names); exercise setup; Screen Time permission and blocking status; and completed workout details such as exercise type, repetitions, duration, and minutes earned. The app may also send Apple Search Ads attribution information when available, such as campaign, ad group, keyword, ad, region, click date, and attribution source. These events and technical details help us understand app use and improve the product.

Current GritMode versions provide all features free. They do not show subscription paywalls or initiate purchases. Some analytics event definitions and historical records concern paywalls, trials, purchases, restores, or premium status from versions 1.0.x; those disclosures apply to data sent by those earlier versions. The 1.1.0+ app does not include RevenueCat and does not send RevenueCat-to-Amplitude subscription data.

Amplitude does not receive the content of your habits, tasks, or notes; HealthKit step data; camera images; or the names or identifiers of apps and websites you select in Screen Time. Exercise details and aggregate app-selection counts are distinct from those excluded data.

GritMode does not use advertising SDKs, IDFA, or cross-app / cross-site tracking for behavioral advertising. Apple Search Ads attribution, when available, is supplied by Apple's AdServices framework and used for campaign measurement through Amplitude.

3.7. Payments and Subscriptions

GritMode 1.1.0 and later are completely free. They have no subscription, free trial, or in-app purchase, and all features are available without an entitlement. The current app binary does not include the RevenueCat SDK. Some users may still have subscriptions purchased through Apple in GritMode 1.0.x. Apple processes those transactions and retains the transaction and subscription records under Apple's policies. Earlier 1.0.x app versions included RevenueCat for subscription entitlement validation and restoration; when those older versions are used, RevenueCat may process the Apple transaction receipt and its pseudonymous app user identifier to provide those legacy functions. Updating to 1.1.0 does not cancel an existing Apple subscription or erase a paid period. We do not receive or store full payment credentials such as bank-card details.

3.8. AI Insights (Optional, Free Feature)

GritMode 1.1.0 and later offer optional AI Insights at no charge. The feature is available after you choose to request a report; the app also records your consent setting. If you do not request a report, the report data described here is not sent. The current app does not require a subscription or RevenueCat entitlement for AI Insights.

When you request a report, the app sends a seven-day aggregated snapshot to our proxy hosted on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.). It includes:

  • counts of completed workout sessions and earned minutes, exercise-type counts, and workout time-of-day groupings;
  • counts of unlock events and minutes granted, grouped by source (workout, bank, or credit) and time of day;
  • the current total bank minutes and debt minutes;
  • counts of Deep Focus sessions and Stop Block uses; and
  • counts of selected blocked apps and websites.

The request also contains your app-language code and a random UUID generated and stored by the app for this installation. The UUID is used as a pseudonymous quota key to limit successful reports to one per installation per day, with additional attempt limits. It is not an Apple ID, account identifier, or RevenueCat ID. The report payload does not include the names or identifiers of selected apps or websites, Screen Time activity records, HealthKit data, camera images, notes, or your name, email address, or Apple ID.

The Worker validates the payload and forwards the aggregate statistics, language code, and requested report instructions to OpenAI through the OpenAI API to generate the report. The installation UUID and the user's network IP are not included in the model request. The returned report is sent to the app and stored locally on your device. We do not store the report or report statistics in our own persistent database.

Cloudflare processes the network request as the hosting provider. The Worker hashes the connecting IP address for a network-level attempt limit and stores that hash and counters, along with the installation quota key, in Cloudflare Workers KV with an expiry of approximately 25 hours; the Worker does not store the raw IP address in KV. Cloudflare may process connection information under its own policies. OpenAI processes report input under its API terms and may retain request content and abuse-monitoring information. OpenAI's published API data controls describe its default restrictions on using API data for model training and its retention practices.

The generated report is cached locally in the app. If you do not use AI Insights again, no further report request is sent.

Earlier app versions (1.0.x) may send a legacy RevenueCat anonymous identifier as their quota key. The Worker accepts that identifier for compatibility; it is not used to check paid access. Section 3.7 explains the separate RevenueCat behavior in those older app versions.
